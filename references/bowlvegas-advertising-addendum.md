# BowlVegas.com Advertising and Local Sponsorship Addendum

**Addendum to:** Event Management Services Agreement  
**Provider:** [Legal provider entity], doing business as BowlVegas.com  
**Client:** Lucky Strike Entertainment Corporation  
**Event:** B.O.B. November 2026  
**Effective date:** [Insert date]

## Purpose

This Advertising and Local Sponsorship Addendum describes an optional process through which BowlVegas.com may collect and organize inquiries from businesses interested in advertising connected with the event or its participant-facing portal materials.

This Addendum **replaces and removes any prior contract language stating that the Client receives 15% of advertising or ad-space sales**. No advertising revenue share, commission, lead volume, sale, sponsorship, or payment is created unless the parties later approve a separate written agreement for a specific transaction.

## 1. Optional advertising opportunity

BowlVegas.com may provide designated advertising or sponsorship placement within available participant-facing areas of the platform or event materials. Possible placements may include portal banners, local-offer areas, event information pages, or other spaces that BowlVegas.com identifies as available.

The availability, size, placement, duration, format, technical requirements, and audience for each placement are controlled by BowlVegas.com’s product and event configuration. A placement may be unavailable for a particular event, device, portal, or publication format.

## 2. Prospect research and introductions

BowlVegas.com may identify prospective local businesses using publicly available business information and may prepare factual outreach drafts or introduction materials. Prospects may include restaurants, transportation companies, entertainment venues, bowling-related businesses, hotels, and other local services that appear relevant to event attendees.

BowlVegas.com will not represent that a prospect has agreed to advertise, that a prospect is suitable for a particular result, or that an advertising placement will produce a specific number of views, clicks, leads, sales, or attendees.

BowlVegas.com will use reasonable care to avoid presenting private attendee information to prospective advertisers. The Client’s roster, contact information, hotel information, QR status, payment information, and other nonpublic attendee data will not be sold or disclosed for advertising purposes under this Addendum.

## 3. Creative approval and content standards

An advertiser must provide advertising content that BowlVegas.com can reasonably display in the available placement. BowlVegas.com may reject, remove, or require changes to content that is unlawful, deceptive, discriminatory, offensive, misleading, incompatible with the event, infringing of another party’s rights, or inconsistent with the platform’s technical requirements.

Neither party will promise an advertiser a result that BowlVegas.com cannot verify or control. Advertising content must identify the advertiser accurately and must not imply that Lucky Strike or BowlVegas.com endorses a product or service unless the parties have approved that endorsement in writing.

## 4. No guaranteed advertising or revenue

BowlVegas.com does not guarantee that an advertiser will purchase a placement or that any prospect will respond to outreach. BowlVegas.com does not guarantee advertising impressions, clicks, leads, conversions, revenue, or return on investment.

The Client acknowledges that advertising availability depends on event timing, creative approval, advertiser interest, technical capacity, participant engagement, and other factors outside BowlVegas.com’s control.

## 5. Fees and revenue sharing

No advertising fee, commission, revenue share, or payment is owed to either party under this Addendum merely because BowlVegas.com researches a prospect, drafts outreach, or displays an available advertising space.

If an advertiser is secured and the parties want to share revenue or assign a fee, the parties must sign a separate written transaction sheet or sponsorship agreement that states at least:

- the advertiser’s legal name;
- the approved placement and event;
- the amount charged to the advertiser;
- any production or placement costs;
- each party’s share or fee;
- invoicing and payment timing;
- cancellation and refund terms; and
- the party responsible for advertiser communication and fulfillment.

No percentage, including the previously referenced 15%, applies unless it appears in that later signed writing.

## 6. Client cooperation

The Client may identify event restrictions, category exclusions, venue restrictions, or sponsor relationships that BowlVegas.com should consider. The Client will promptly notify BowlVegas.com if the Client lacks authority to approve an advertising placement or if the venue, bowling center, league, or another partner controls the relevant advertising space.

The Client will not be responsible for paying an advertiser’s invoice unless the Client separately agrees in writing to act as the contracting advertiser representative or payment recipient.

## 7. Data and reporting

Any reporting provided to the Client will be limited to information that BowlVegas.com can reasonably collect from the placement or outreach process. Reports may include the advertiser name, placement status, campaign dates, and available aggregate activity. Reports will not include private attendee identities or personal contact information unless the attendee has independently and lawfully requested contact from the advertiser and the parties have approved the applicable process.

## 8. Term and termination

This Addendum applies only to the event and advertising placements identified in a signed transaction sheet or written approval. Either party may withdraw from a proposed advertising opportunity before the advertiser transaction is signed. Termination of this Addendum does not terminate the underlying Event Management Services Agreement unless the parties expressly state otherwise.

## 9. Relationship to the Event Management Services Agreement

Except as expressly changed by this Addendum, the Event Management Services Agreement remains in effect. If this Addendum conflicts with the prior 15% advertising language or any other advertising provision in the underlying agreement, this Addendum controls and the prior 15% language is deleted and has no effect.
