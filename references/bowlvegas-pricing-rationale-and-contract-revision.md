# BowlVegas.com Pricing Rationale and Contract Revision Memo

**Prepared for:** Business-partner review  
**Client context:** Lucky Strike Entertainment Corporation, represented by Cassie Davis  
**Service:** BowlVegas.com event-management and registration platform  
**Status:** Internal business discussion document; not legal advice

## Executive recommendation

BowlVegas.com should move away from the original **$299 setup fee plus $1.99 per bowler** because that price does not adequately compensate the business for the operational work and responsibility required to deliver a reliable event system.

The approved pricing structure is:

- **Lucky Strike launch rate for the B.O.B. November event:** **$399 setup fee plus $2.49 per unique registered bowler**.
- **Future standard rate:** **$499 setup fee plus $2.99 per unique registered bowler**.

The launch rate gives Lucky Strike a meaningful first-event accommodation while establishing that the normal value of the service is higher. The per-bowler charge should apply only to unique bowlers who complete registration. Guests, coordinators, Event Director staff, doormen, duplicate records, and internal test records should not be included in the bowler count unless the parties later agree to a separate service.

## Why the price increase is necessary

### 1. The service is more than software access

BowlVegas.com is not simply providing a login or a generic software subscription. Each event requires event-specific preparation, configuration, quality control, and support. The work includes configuring the event, organizing the roster, generating claim codes and QR materials, preparing printable distribution materials, supporting the bowler and captain portals, and setting up the Event Director’s operational workspace.

The setup fee compensates BowlVegas.com for this front-loaded work. A low setup fee creates a mismatch between the actual labor required and the amount recovered before the event begins.

### 2. The platform supports multiple operational roles

The system serves bowlers, team captains, Event Directors, coordinators, and doorman or check-in staff. Each role has different access boundaries and responsibilities. Configuring those workflows and verifying that they operate together requires more work than a single-user application.

The pricing therefore reflects a managed event system rather than a basic self-service form.

### 3. QR, claim-code, and check-in operations create real event responsibility

The platform generates event-specific claim codes and QR materials, supports personal participant access, and provides check-in tools for banquet and pool-party operations. QR redemption and single-use controls must be prepared carefully because a configuration error can affect entry at the event.

The service also includes operational planning for scanner stations, verification displays, offline procedures, and fallback processes. These responsibilities justify pricing above a basic roster or registration tool.

### 4. Data preparation and integration require ongoing attention

Each event may involve a Google Sheet or other roster source with different layouts, missing fields, multiple leagues, center assignments, team data, hotel information, seating details, and special requests. The system must map source data into the correct event structure while protecting app-managed fields such as claim codes, QR data, and redemption status.

The per-bowler fee reflects the variable work and operational scale created by each registered participant. It also aligns the cost with the size of the event rather than charging every client the same amount regardless of roster size.

### 5. Event-day support carries risk that is not visible in the setup screen

A reliable event system must be supported when people are actively checking in. Technical issues can involve a browser, device, scanner, network, Google Sheets permission, event configuration, duplicate scan, invalid token, or offline synchronization. Even when no issue occurs, the provider must remain prepared to respond.

The price must therefore account for availability, troubleshooting, contingency planning, and the business risk of supporting a live event.

### 6. The investment in the system should be recovered responsibly

BowlVegas.com reflects significant investment in development, testing, database design, security controls, offline scanning, event workflows, printed artifacts, and ongoing maintenance. Pricing below the value of the delivered service delays recovery of that investment and limits the ability to fund future improvements, support, backups, security work, and customer service.

The increase is not intended to charge for features that are unavailable or to promise outcomes that the platform cannot guarantee. It is intended to establish a sustainable price for the work that is actually delivered.

## Why the launch rate is strategically useful

The launch rate provides a reasonable way to close the Lucky Strike engagement without weakening the long-term price position. It can be described as a **first-event launch rate** or **founding-client rate**. The agreement should state that it applies only to the defined B.O.B. November engagement and does not establish the price for future events.

A discount should be tied to a clear business reason. For example, the launch rate may be offered in exchange for timely planning participation, permission to use non-confidential operational feedback, and the opportunity to discuss future events. The contract should not require a public testimonial or marketing approval unless Lucky Strike separately agrees in writing.

## Contract changes approved for the next revision

The next contract version should make the following changes:

1. Use **BowlVegas.com** as the public-facing brand.
2. Identify the correct legal contracting entity. If Nexus Peak LLC remains the owner, use “Nexus Peak LLC, doing business as BowlVegas.com.”
3. Replace the original pricing with **$399 plus $2.49 per unique registered bowler** for the B.O.B. November launch event.
4. State that the future standard rate is **$499 plus $2.99 per unique registered bowler**.
5. Define which records are included in the bowler count and exclude guests, coordinators, staff, doormen, duplicates, and test records.
6. Remove the 15% advertising revenue language from the event-services agreement.
7. Replace that language with a separate advertising addendum that creates no guaranteed sale, lead volume, revenue, or payment.
8. Resolve the existing date, cancellation, data-retention, Google Sheets, support, privacy, and signature inconsistencies before sending the revised agreement for signature.

## Suggested partner-facing explanation

> BowlVegas.com’s pricing reflects a managed event service rather than a basic software subscription. The service includes event configuration, roster preparation, claim-code and QR generation, participant and captain portals, Event Director tools, check-in workflows, training, offline and contingency preparation, and event-day technical support. The $399 setup fee and $2.49 per unique registered bowler launch rate provide Lucky Strike with a first-event accommodation while allowing BowlVegas.com to recover a reasonable portion of the work required to prepare and support the event. Future events will use the standard rate of $499 plus $2.99 per unique registered bowler. The pricing excludes guests, coordinators, staff, duplicate records, and internal test records from the bowler count.

This explanation is intentionally factual. It does not promise perfect uptime, guaranteed attendance, guaranteed savings, guaranteed revenue, or elimination of all manual event work.
