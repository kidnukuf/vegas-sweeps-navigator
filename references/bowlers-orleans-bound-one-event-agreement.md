## Services included

Subject to the limitations in this Agreement, the Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to provide the following services for the Event:

### Event configuration

The Provider will configure the Event in BowlVegas.com, establish the applicable participant and staff roles, and prepare the event-specific settings reasonably necessary for the agreed scope.

### Roster preparation and data import

The Provider will assist with importing or preparing Client-supplied roster information. The Parties may use a designated Google Sheet, CSV file, or other agreed source. The Client remains responsible for the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, and lawful collection of the source information.

The Provider may map source fields into the event structure and may identify obvious formatting or completeness issues. The Provider does not guarantee that a source file is accurate merely because it can be imported.

### Claim codes and QR materials

The Provider will generate event-specific claim codes and QR materials for eligible registered participants according to the agreed event workflow. The Provider may prepare downloadable or printable materials, including roster or admission materials, when those materials are included in the event plan.

Claim codes and QR codes are operational credentials. The Client will distribute them only to the intended participant or authorized recipient and will promptly report suspected duplication, disclosure, or misuse.

### Participant and staff portals

The configured service may include access for bowlers, team captains, coordinators, Event Directors, owners, and check-in staff, depending on the agreed event configuration. Access is role-based. The Client will designate personnel who need operational access and will promptly request removal of access when a person no longer needs it.

### Check-in and scanning operations

The Provider will configure the agreed QR check-in workflow, including event-specific validation and single-use controls where supported. If the event uses multiple scanner stations, each station may have its own display or station identifier while remaining subject to the event’s shared redemption rules.

The Provider may provide offline or contingency instructions when those procedures are part of the event plan. Offline operation depends on the supported hardware, browser, local storage, scanner configuration, power, and synchronization process. The Provider does not guarantee uninterrupted operation of any third-party scanner, computer, network, browser, operating system, or internet service.

### Training and documentation

The Provider will provide reasonable orientation or written instructions for the designated Client personnel regarding the configured workflows. The Client is responsible for ensuring that Event Directors, coordinators, doormen, and other staff follow the instructions and maintain the required devices and access credentials.

### Post-event export and debrief

The Provider will make the Client’s event data available through the configured platform or an agreed export process after the Event, subject to the retention terms below. The Parties may conduct a debrief consultation if included in the event schedule or otherwise agreed in writing.

## Google Sheets and third-party services

The Client will provide the designated Google Sheet, event tab, source file, and permissions necessary for the agreed import and write-back operations. The Client will maintain its Google account, sharing permissions, source data, and internal approval process.

The Provider may write only app-managed fields and other fields expressly approved by the Client. The Client remains responsible for reviewing imported data and any written-back data before relying on it for reservations, staffing, payment, communications, or other operational decisions.

The Provider is not responsible for an outage, permission error, API change, rate limit, data loss, delay, or other failure caused by Google, a spreadsheet owner, a third-party API, a network, a device, or a service outside the Provider’s reasonable control. The Provider will use reasonable efforts to identify and communicate a configuration issue when it becomes aware of one.

## Client responsibilities

The Client will:

1. provide accurate and lawfully collected event, roster, contact, hotel, seating, team, and other operational information;
2. provide source data and material corrections by the agreed deadlines;
3. identify the Event Director, coordinators, check-in staff, and other authorized users;
4. ensure that staff complete the available orientation and follow the documented procedures;
5. provide suitable devices, power, scanners, browsers, network access, and physical operating space unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing;
6. distribute claim codes and QR materials securely and only to intended recipients;
7. review participant data, generated materials, event settings, and check-in rules before the Event;
8. promptly report inaccurate data, suspected account compromise, lost credentials, duplicated codes, and operational issues; and
9. obtain all permissions, notices, consents, and approvals required for the Client’s collection and use of participant information.

The Client will not represent that BowlVegas.com guarantees attendance, event revenue, perfect uptime, elimination of manual work, or a particular business result.

## Pricing and payment

### Lucky Strike launch pricing

For the Bowlers Orleans Bound identified in Section 1, the Client will pay:

- **Setup and event-configuration fee:** **$199.00**; and
- **Platform fee:** **$1.99 for each unique bowler who completes registration for the Event**.

This is a one-event new-client acquisition special. It applies only to Bowlers Orleans Bound and does not establish the standard price for future events.

### Future standard pricing

Unless the Parties agree to a different written proposal, future events will be priced at:

- **Setup and event-configuration fee:** **$499.00**; and
- **Platform fee:** **$2.99 for each unique bowler who completes registration for the applicable Event**.

### Bowler-count rules

The platform fee applies to a unique bowler who completes registration for the Event. Guests, coordinators, Event Director staff, doormen, duplicate accounts, internal test records, and other non-bowler records are excluded from the bowler count unless the Parties approve a separate written fee.

The Parties will use the Provider’s event records, reasonably reconciled against the Client’s final roster, to determine the billable count. A duplicate account that is merged or removed before final reconciliation is not billed as a second bowler. The Parties should identify in writing any special treatment for a bowler who registers and later cancels.

### Invoicing and payment

The Provider may invoice the Client within three business days after the Event or after the final billable count is reasonably available. Payment is due within **30 days** after the invoice date. The Client will notify the Provider of a good-faith invoice dispute within 15 days after receipt and will pay all undisputed amounts when due.

Work outside the included scope, including substantial additional data repair, additional event dates, additional support days, custom materials, or custom integrations, requires written approval and may be billed at **$75 per hour** unless the Parties agree to a fixed price.

## Support and service limitations

The Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to respond to operational issues during the agreed event support window. Support is subject to the availability of the Provider’s personnel and the Client’s timely provision of information needed to diagnose an issue.

The Provider does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free service. The service may depend on third-party hosting, internet access, Google services, devices, scanners, browsers, power, local storage, and user actions. The Client is responsible for maintaining a practical paper or manual fallback process for critical admission operations.

The Provider is not responsible for an issue caused by inaccurate Client data, unauthorized changes, unsupported hardware, a third-party outage, a Client-controlled account, a lost device, a failure to follow instructions, or an event condition outside the Provider’s reasonable control.

## Data ownership, privacy, and security

As between the Parties, the Client owns the Client’s event data supplied to or generated for the Event, subject to the Provider’s ownership of the platform, software, templates, workflows, know-how, and generalized improvements.

Each Party will use personal information only for event administration, registration, communications, check-in, safety, support, billing, compliance, or another documented purpose related to the Event. Neither Party will sell the Client’s participant roster or disclose nonpublic participant contact information to advertisers under this Agreement or the Advertising Addendum.

Each Party will use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information it controls. The Parties will cooperate reasonably in investigating a confirmed security incident affecting the Client’s event data. The Provider will notify the Client without unreasonable delay after confirming a security incident that materially affects the Client’s event data, subject to legal and security constraints.

The Client is responsible for the lawfulness of its source data, notices, consents, communications, and instructions. The Client will not upload information that it is not authorized to provide.

## Data retention and deletion

The Client may request deletion of live event data within 30 days after the Event. Unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing, the Provider will make live event data available for **12 months after the Event**. After that period, the Provider may delete or anonymize the data, subject to legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, backup, and disaster-recovery requirements.

Backup copies may remain temporarily in ordinary backup cycles after deletion from live systems. The Provider may retain anonymized aggregate information that does not identify the Client or an individual participant for security, service improvement, operational analysis, or reporting.

## Intellectual property and use rights

The Provider retains all rights in BowlVegas.com, its software, source code, platform architecture, templates, workflows, documentation, generalized know-how, and non-identifying improvements. The Client retains its rights in its name, logos, event content, roster data, and other materials it supplies.

During the term of this Agreement, the Provider grants the Client a limited, nonexclusive, nontransferable right to use the configured BowlVegas.com event materials and event exports for the Event and the Client’s internal event administration. The Client may print and distribute participant materials for the Event but may not resell, reverse engineer, copy, or commercially exploit the platform.

## Advertising and sponsorship

Any advertising or local-sponsorship arrangement is governed by the **BowlVegas.com Advertising and Local Sponsorship Addendum** attached to this Agreement. The Addendum replaces any prior statement that the Client receives 15% of advertising or ad-space sales.

No advertising sale, lead volume, revenue share, commission, payment, or return on investment is guaranteed. No percentage, including 15%, applies unless the Parties approve a separate written transaction agreement for a specific advertiser and placement.

## Cancellation, postponement, and termination

If the Client cancels before the Provider begins material setup work, the setup fee is refundable. Once material setup work begins, the setup fee is nonrefundable because it compensates the Provider for work performed. No per-bowler platform fee is due for a bowler who never completes registration, subject to the bowler-count rules in Section 5.

If the Client postpones the Event, the Parties will cooperate in good faith to apply completed setup work to the replacement date. A material change in scope, timing, roster size, support window, or technical requirements may require a written price adjustment.

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for a material breach that remains uncured for 15 days after written notice. The Provider may suspend access immediately when reasonably necessary to protect security, prevent misuse, or comply with law. Termination does not eliminate payment obligations for services already performed or undisputed amounts already due.

## Confidentiality

Each Party may receive nonpublic business, technical, financial, or participant information from the other Party. The receiving Party will use that information only for the Event and will protect it with reasonable care. Confidential information does not include information that is publicly available without breach, already lawfully known, independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.

A Party may disclose confidential information when required by law, provided that it gives advance notice when legally permitted and reasonably cooperates with efforts to limit the disclosure.

## Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party will be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, lost business, or loss of anticipated savings arising from this Agreement.

Except for a Party’s fraud, willful misconduct, intentional misuse of confidential information, or obligations that cannot lawfully be limited, each Party’s aggregate liability arising from this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable for the specific Event giving rise to the claim.

If the platform is materially unavailable during a scheduled support window due solely to the Provider’s negligence, the Client’s exclusive service remedy is a refund of the $199 new-client acquisition setup fee for the affected Event, unless the Parties agree in writing to another credit or remedy. This remedy does not apply to a third-party outage, Client-controlled system, unsupported hardware, inaccurate Client data, or a condition outside the Provider’s reasonable control.

## Force majeure and third-party outages

Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, fire, flood, epidemic, labor disruption, government action, civil disturbance, power failure, internet failure, hosting failure, cyberattack by an unrelated third party, Google or API outage, scanner or device failure, or other force-majeure event.

The affected Party will provide reasonable notice when practical and will use reasonable efforts to resume performance. The Client remains responsible for maintaining a manual or paper fallback process for critical event admission.

## Dispute resolution and governing law

The Parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve a dispute through direct discussion between authorized representatives. If the dispute is not resolved, the Parties will attempt confidential mediation in Clark County, Nevada before filing a court action, except where immediate injunctive relief is reasonably necessary.

This Agreement is governed by Nevada law, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. Subject to the mediation requirement, the state and federal courts located in Clark County, Nevada, have exclusive jurisdiction.

# BOWLVEGAS.COM ADVERTISING AND LOCAL SPONSORSHIP ADDENDUM

## A-1. Purpose

This Addendum describes an optional process through which BowlVegas.com may identify and organize inquiries from businesses interested in advertising connected with the Event or participant-facing portal materials.

This Addendum **replaces and removes any prior contract language stating that the Client receives 15% of advertising or ad-space sales**. No advertising revenue share, commission, lead volume, sale, sponsorship, or payment is created unless the Parties later approve a separate written agreement for a specific transaction.

## A-2. Optional advertising opportunities

BowlVegas.com may provide designated advertising or sponsorship placement in available participant-facing areas of the platform or Event materials. Possible placements may include portal banners, local-offer areas, event information pages, or other spaces that BowlVegas.com identifies as available.

Availability, size, placement, duration, format, technical requirements, and audience are controlled by BowlVegas.com’s product and event configuration. A placement may be unavailable for a particular Event, device, portal, or publication format.

## A-3. Prospect research and introductions

BowlVegas.com may identify prospective local businesses using publicly available business information and may prepare factual outreach drafts or introduction materials. Prospects may include restaurants, transportation companies, entertainment venues, bowling-related businesses, hotels, and other local services relevant to Event attendees.

BowlVegas.com will not represent that a prospect has agreed to advertise, that a prospect is suitable for a particular result, or that an advertising placement will produce a specific number of views, clicks, leads, sales, or attendees.

BowlVegas.com will use reasonable care to avoid presenting private attendee information to prospective advertisers. The Client’s roster, contact information, hotel information, QR status, payment information, and other nonpublic attendee data will not be sold or disclosed for advertising purposes under this Addendum.

## A-4. Creative approval and content standards

An advertiser must provide content that BowlVegas.com can reasonably display in the available placement. BowlVegas.com may reject, remove, or require changes to content that is unlawful, deceptive, discriminatory, offensive, misleading, incompatible with the Event, infringing of another party’s rights, or inconsistent with technical requirements.

Advertising content must identify the advertiser accurately and must not imply that Lucky Strike or BowlVegas.com endorses a product or service unless the Parties approve that endorsement in writing.

## A-5. No guaranteed advertising or revenue

BowlVegas.com does not guarantee that an advertiser will purchase a placement or that any prospect will respond to outreach. BowlVegas.com does not guarantee advertising impressions, clicks, leads, conversions, revenue, or return on investment.

The Client acknowledges that advertising availability depends on Event timing, creative approval, advertiser interest, technical capacity, participant engagement, and other factors outside BowlVegas.com’s control.

## A-6. Fees and revenue sharing

No advertising fee, commission, revenue share, or payment is owed to either Party under this Addendum merely because BowlVegas.com researches a prospect, drafts outreach, or displays an available advertising space.

If an advertiser is secured and the Parties want to share revenue or assign a fee, the Parties must sign a separate written transaction sheet or sponsorship agreement that states:

- the advertiser’s legal name;
- the approved placement and Event;
- the amount charged to the advertiser;
- production or placement costs;
- each Party’s share or fee;
- invoicing and payment timing;
- cancellation and refund terms; and
- the Party responsible for advertiser communication and fulfillment.

No percentage, including the previously referenced 15%, applies unless it appears in that later signed writing.

## A-7. Client cooperation

The Client may identify event restrictions, category exclusions, venue restrictions, or sponsor relationships that BowlVegas.com should consider. The Client will promptly notify BowlVegas.com if the Client lacks authority to approve an advertising placement or if the venue, bowling center, league, or another partner controls the relevant advertising space.

The Client will not be responsible for paying an advertiser’s invoice unless the Client separately agrees in writing to act as the contracting advertiser representative or payment recipient.

## A-8. Data and reporting

Any report provided to the Client will be limited to information that BowlVegas.com can reasonably collect from the placement or outreach process. Reports may include the advertiser name, placement status, campaign dates, and available aggregate activity. Reports will not include private attendee identities or personal contact information unless the attendee has independently and lawfully requested contact from the advertiser and the Parties have approved the applicable process.

## A-9. Term and termination

This Addendum applies only to the Event and advertising placements identified in a signed transaction sheet or written approval. Either Party may withdraw from a proposed advertising opportunity before the advertiser transaction is signed. Termination of this Addendum does not terminate the underlying Event Management Services Agreement unless the Parties expressly state otherwise.

## A-10. Relationship to the Agreement

Except as expressly changed by this Addendum, the Event Management Services Agreement remains in effect. If this Addendum conflicts with prior 15% advertising language or another advertising provision in the underlying Agreement, this Addendum controls and the prior 15% language is deleted and has no effect.
